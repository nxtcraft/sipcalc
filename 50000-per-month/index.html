Step-Up SIP: Raise ₹50,000 by 10% Every Year

If you start at ₹50,000 a month and increase it by 10% each year (₹55,000 in year 2, and so on), after 20 years at 12% you would have about ₹9,94,43,577, versus ₹4,99,57,396 with a flat SIP. With the step-up you reach ₹1 crore in about 8 years instead of 10. Step-up SIP calculator →

Tax on ₹50,000 SIP Returns

For equity mutual funds held over a year, long-term gains above ₹1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at 12.5%. If you withdrew the whole 20-year gain of ₹3,79,57,396 in one year, the tax would be about ₹47,29,049. Spreading withdrawals across years uses the ₹1.25 lakh exemption each year and lowers this. Units held under a year are taxed at 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What will a ₹50,000 SIP become in 10 years?

About ₹1,16,16,954 at 12% a year, from ₹60,00,000 invested. At 10% it would be ₹1,03,27,601; at 15%, ₹1,39,32,864.

What will a ₹50,000 SIP become in 20 years?

About ₹4,99,57,396 at 12% a year, from ₹1,20,00,000 invested. With a 10% yearly step-up it would be about ₹9,94,43,577.

How long does a ₹50,000 SIP take to reach ₹1 crore?

About 10 years at 12% a year, or 8 years if you raise the SIP by 10% every year.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. 12% is an assumption for planning. Equity mutual fund returns vary from year to year and can be negative over short periods.
