What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month — like an EMI, but for wealth creation. You don't need to time the market. Over time, rupee cost averaging smooths out market volatility.

💡 ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% return = ₹49.96 lakhs — from just ₹12 lakhs invested.

SIP Formula

M = P × [(1+r)ⁿ − 1] ÷ r × (1+r)

  • P = Monthly SIP amount
  • r = Monthly rate = Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • n = Months = Years × 12

Expected Returns by Fund Type

  • Large Cap funds: 10–11% (stable, lower risk)
  • Flexi/Multi Cap funds: 11–13% (balanced)
  • Mid Cap funds: 12–15% (higher growth, higher risk)
  • Debt funds: 6–8% (low risk, good for short term)
  • Index funds (Nifty 50): ~11–12% long-term average

Frequently Asked Questions

How much SIP is needed for ₹1 crore?

At 12% annual return: ₹10,000/month for 20 years → ₹99.9 lakhs. ₹5,500/month for 25 years → ₹1.02 crore. Use the Goal Planner tab above for your exact target.

Is SIP better than FD?

For long term (5+ years), SIP in equity mutual funds typically outperforms FD. FD gives ~6–7% (taxable), while equity SIP has historically returned 10–13% over 10+ year periods. SIP is not guaranteed — equity markets can fall. FD is fully guaranteed.

Can I stop SIP anytime?

Yes. Most mutual funds allow you to pause or stop SIP at any time with no penalty. You can also withdraw (redeem) your accumulated units at any time (subject to exit load if within 1 year for equity funds).

What is Step-Up SIP?

Step-Up SIP means increasing your SIP amount every year (e.g., by 10%). This accelerates wealth creation significantly. If your salary grows, stepping up your SIP by even 5–10% yearly can dramatically increase your final corpus.
