Frequently Asked Questions

How is SIP maturity calculated?

Future value = P × ((1+r)^n − 1) ÷ r × (1+r), where P is the monthly amount, r the monthly return (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of months.

How much SIP is needed for ₹1 crore in 20 years?

At 12% a year, about ₹10100 a month.
